The ground rules

Clear rules keep things fair for every partner. These are written into your agreement.

  1. This is a commission-based freelance role. There is no salary, and there is no fee to join.
  2. Register every lead before you pitch. The first partner to register a business owns it for 60 days.
  3. Existing Core 8 Media clients and leads already with us are not eligible. We share a "do not pitch" list.
  4. You introduce and book the meeting. Only Core 8 Media quotes final prices, gives discounts or signs agreements.
  5. Commission is paid after the client pays, by the 10th of the following month. If a client is refunded in the first month, that month's commission is reversed.
  6. Every partner signs a Sales Partner Agreement and an NDA before starting. Client information stays confidential.

Questions

Still unsure about something? Message us on WhatsApp.

Do I need to pay anything to join?

No. Joining is free. You never pay Core 8 Media anything.

Is there a fixed salary or a target?

No salary and no target. You earn only commission and bonuses on clients you bring who pay us.

Do I have to close the deal myself?

No. You make the introduction and book the meeting. Our team handles the proposal, pricing and closing.

Can I do this alongside my job or studies?

Yes. Most partners do this part-time. Check that it doesn't conflict with your current employer's rules.

How and when do I get paid?

By bank transfer, by the 10th of each month, for clients who paid in the previous month. You get a statement with every payout. TDS is deducted as per law, so we'll need your PAN.

What if a client stays longer than 3 months?

Retainer commission runs for the first 3 months of each client. If the client stays with us for 6 months, you also get a ₹2,000 retention bonus.

Can I work from outside Bengaluru?

Yes. Most clients are in Bengaluru, but we work with businesses across India and meetings can happen online.

What happens after I apply?

If you're shortlisted, our team calls you within 48 hours for a short chat. Selected partners attend an online onboarding session, sign the agreement and NDA, and get a starter kit with our portfolio, pitch script and lead form.
